Privacy Policy for Hue Sort: Color Puzzle

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Hue Sort: Color Puzzle ("the App") is a free puzzle game developed by Elkassas ("we", "us").
Contact: m.elkassas95@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

The App itself does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or any personal details. Game progress (coins, stars, unlocked levels, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

2. Advertising

The App shows rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one (for an Extra Tube, extra Undos, a free Hint, or Double Coins). The App shows no banner ads and no interstitial ads.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • the device's advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in Android Settings, under Privacy, then Ads),
  • device information (model, operating system, language),
  • approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address,
  • app interaction and diagnostic data.

Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3. Consent (EEA/UK)

Where required, ads are served in accordance with applicable consent rules for personalized advertising.

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data processed by Google AdMob is handled as described in Google's policies above.
